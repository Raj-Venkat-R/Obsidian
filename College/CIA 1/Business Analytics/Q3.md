# Analytics / Business Analytics Life Cycle (Laymen Format)

## Introduction

The **Analytics Life Cycle** is a step-by-step process used to solve business problems using data. It helps organizations understand problems clearly, analyze data properly, and make better decisions.

Both questions (Q13 and Q24) ask about:
- Stages/Steps of analytics life cycle  
- Tools used  
- Business examples  
- Summary  

So this common answer can be written for both.

---

# Stages of Analytics Life Cycle (6 Marks)

### 1. Business Understanding

In this stage, we clearly understand the business problem.

✔ What is the problem?  
✔ What is the goal?  
✔ What does the company expect?

**Example:**  
A retail company notices sales are decreasing.

---

### 2. Data Understanding

Collect and study the data related to the problem.

✔ Where is the data stored?  
✔ Is the data complete?  
✔ What type of data is available?

**Example:**  
Company collects sales data, customer feedback, region-wise reports.

---

### 3. Data Preparation

Clean and organize the data.

✔ Remove missing values  
✔ Remove duplicate data  
✔ Convert data into proper format  

This step is very important because raw data is usually messy.

**Example:**  
Remove incorrect sales entries and format dates properly.

---

### 4. Model Building (Data Modeling)

Use statistical or machine learning techniques to analyze data and find patterns.

✔ Regression  
✔ Classification  
✔ Clustering  

**Example:**  
Use regression to predict future sales.

---

### 5. Evaluation

Check whether the model gives correct and useful results.

✔ Is the prediction accurate?  
✔ Does it solve the business problem?

If not, go back and improve the model.

---

### 6. Deployment (Implementation)

Apply the solution in real business environment.

✔ Use dashboards  
✔ Automate reports  
✔ Implement prediction system  

**Example:**  
Company uses a dashboard to monitor daily sales.

---

# Business Use Case for Each Stage (6 Marks)

Let us take an example of an **Online Shopping Company**.

### Stage 1 – Business Understanding  
Problem: Customers are leaving the platform.

### Stage 2 – Data Understanding  
Collect data on customer purchases, delivery time, complaints.

### Stage 3 – Data Preparation  
Clean customer records and remove incomplete entries.

### Stage 4 – Model Building  
Build a churn prediction model to identify customers likely to leave.

### Stage 5 – Evaluation  
Check accuracy of the churn model.

### Stage 6 – Deployment  
Send special discount offers to customers who may leave.

Result: Customer retention improves.

# Summary (3 Marks)

The Analytics Life Cycle is a structured process that converts raw data into useful business decisions. It includes business understanding, data collection, preparation, modeling, evaluation, and deployment. By following this cycle, companies can reduce risk, improve performance, and make data-driven decisions.  

In simple words, analytics life cycle helps businesses move from **problem → data → analysis → solution → improvement**.
