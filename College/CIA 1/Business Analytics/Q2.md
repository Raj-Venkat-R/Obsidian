# Decision Making Process and Role of Business Intelligence (BI)

## Introduction

Decision making is the process of selecting the best option among different alternatives to solve a problem or achieve a goal. In business, good decision making helps organizations improve performance, reduce risks, and increase profits.

Business Intelligence (BI) systems support decision making by collecting, analyzing, and presenting data in an understandable format.

---

# Part 1: Steps in Decision Making (3 Marks)

The basic steps in decision making are:

1. **Identify the Problem**  
   Recognize that there is an issue or opportunity that needs attention.

2. **Evaluate Alternatives**  
   Analyze different possible solutions.

3. **Select the Best Option**  
   Choose the most suitable solution based on data and analysis.

---

# Part 2: Detailed Decision-Making Process (7 Marks)

### 1. Problem Identification  
The first step is clearly defining the problem.  
Example: “Sales are decreasing in the last quarter.”

### 2. Data Collection  
Gather relevant data related to the problem.  
Example: Sales reports, customer feedback, competitor pricing.

### 3. Analysis of Data  
Study the collected data to find patterns and causes.  
Example: Sales are low mainly in one region.

### 4. Generate Alternatives  
Think of possible solutions.  
- Reduce price  
- Increase marketing  
- Improve product quality  

### 5. Evaluate Alternatives  
Compare advantages and disadvantages of each option.

### 6. Choose the Best Alternative  
Select the most effective solution based on evidence.

### 7. Implementation and Monitoring  
Apply the decision and continuously monitor results.

---

# Part 3: Decision Making Supported by Business Intelligence (Theory – 6 Marks)

Business Intelligence (BI) systems help organizations make informed decisions using data.

### 1. Data-Driven Decisions  
BI tools provide real-time and historical data to support decisions.

### 2. Faster Decision Making  
Dashboards and reports help managers quickly understand business performance.

### 3. Improved Accuracy  
Decisions are based on analyzed data instead of guesswork.

### 4. Better Forecasting  
BI systems help predict future trends using past data.

### 5. Performance Monitoring  
Managers can track KPIs (Key Performance Indicators).

### 6. Strategic Planning  
BI supports long-term planning through trend analysis and forecasting.

---

# Part 4: BI Tools Used in Decision Making (Tools – 5 Marks)

Some common Business Intelligence tools include:

1. **Microsoft Power BI** – Used for creating interactive dashboards and reports.
2. **Tableau** – Used for advanced data visualization.
3. **SAP BusinessObjects** – Used for enterprise reporting and analytics.
4. **Google Looker Studio** – Used for real-time data visualization.
5. **SQL & Data Warehousing Systems** – Used to store and manage large business data.

These tools collect data from multiple sources, process it, and present it in charts, graphs, and dashboards.

---

# Part 5: Use Case Example (4 Marks)

### Example: Retail Company

A retail company notices a drop in profits.

### Step 1: BI Dashboard  
The manager checks the Power BI dashboard.

### Step 2: Analysis  
The dashboard shows:
- Sales are low in one region.
- Inventory costs are high.
- Customer return rate increased.

### Step 3: Decision  
The company decides to:
- Offer discounts in that region.
- Reduce excess inventory.
- Improve product quality.

### Result  
After implementation, sales increase and profits improve.

---

# Conclusion

Decision making is a systematic process that involves identifying problems, analyzing alternatives, and selecting the best solution. Business Intelligence systems enhance this process by providing accurate data, visual insights, and predictive analysis. With BI support, organizations can make faster, smarter, and more effective decisions.
