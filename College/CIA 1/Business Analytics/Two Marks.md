# Two-Mark Answers (Laymen Format)

---

### 1. Define Data Science in the context of Business Analytics.

Data Science in business analytics means using data, statistics, and technology to understand business problems and make better decisions. It helps companies find patterns and predict future outcomes.

---

### 2. Mention two activities in the modeling phase of analytics.

1. Selecting a suitable model (like regression or classification).  
2. Training the model using data to make predictions.

---

### 3. Differentiate between Business Problem and Analytics Problem.

- **Business Problem:** Real-world issue faced by company.  
  Example: Sales are decreasing.

- **Analytics Problem:** Converting business problem into a data-based question.  
  Example: Which factors are affecting sales decline?

---

### 4. What are the goals of validation in analytics modeling?

- To check whether the model is accurate.  
- To ensure the model works properly on new data.

---

### 5. List any two challenges in deployment of analytics models.

1. Integrating model with existing systems.  
2. Maintaining model performance over time.

---

### 6. What is the importance of interpretation in analytics?

Interpretation helps managers understand the results of analysis clearly so they can make proper business decisions.

---

### 7. Define a Data Warehouse and mention its key characteristics.

A Data Warehouse is a central storage system that stores large amounts of organized data for analysis.

Key characteristics:
- Integrated (combines data from different sources)  
- Historical (stores past data)  
- Subject-oriented (organized by topic like sales or finance)

---

### 8. What is the role of OLAP cubes in analytics?

OLAP cubes help in analyzing data from different angles quickly, like region-wise, product-wise, or time-wise analysis.

---

### 9. Distinguish between Tactical and Operational Decisions.

- **Tactical Decisions:** Short-term decisions made by middle management.  
  Example: Planning monthly marketing campaign.

- **Operational Decisions:** Daily routine decisions.  
  Example: Managing daily employee schedules.

---

### 10. List any two functions of a Decision Support System (DSS).

1. Provides reports and analysis.  
2. Helps managers compare different decision options.

---

### 14. Define Business Analytics.

Business Analytics is the process of analyzing data to improve business performance and support decision making.

---

### 15. Mention the phases in the Analytics Life Cycle.

1. Business Understanding  
2. Data Understanding  
3. Data Preparation  
4. Modeling  
5. Evaluation  
6. Deployment  

---

### 16. Distinguish between Descriptive and Predictive Analytics.

- **Descriptive Analytics:** Explains what happened in the past.  
- **Predictive Analytics:** Predicts what may happen in the future.

---

### 17. What is the purpose of Data Preparation?

Data preparation is done to clean and organize raw data so it can be used properly for analysis.

---

### 18. Define Hypothesis Generation in analytics.

Hypothesis generation means forming an assumption or educated guess about a problem that can be tested using data.

---

### 19. List two types of data models used in analytics.

1. Regression Model  
2. Classification Model  

---

### 20. What are the advantages of using Data Marts over Data Warehouses?

1. Smaller and faster to access.  
2. Focused on a specific department (like sales or HR).

---

### 21. Define OLAP. Mention one application.

OLAP (Online Analytical Processing) is a system used to analyze large amounts of data from multiple dimensions.

Application: Sales performance analysis.

---

### 22. Differentiate between Operational and Strategic Decisions.

- **Operational Decisions:** Daily short-term decisions.  
- **Strategic Decisions:** Long-term decisions made by top management.

---

### 23. Define Knowledge Management and its role in Business Intelligence.

Knowledge Management is the process of collecting and sharing important business information.

In Business Intelligence, it helps organizations use past data and experience to make better decisions.
