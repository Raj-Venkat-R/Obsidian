## Part – A (2 Marks Questions and Answers)

### 1. List any two applications of industrial engineering.
Industrial engineering is applied in  
i. Manufacturing  
ii. Healthcare  
iii. Logistics and Supply Chain  
iv. Service Industries  
v. Technology and Automation  
vi. Energy and Sustainability.  

### 2. Define productivity Or What is productivity?
Productivity is the ratio of output to input. It measures how efficiently the resources are utilized.  

### 3. What is the difference between Production Management and Industrial Engineering?

Feature | Production Management | Industrial Engineering  
Definition | Manages manufacturing processes. | Improves systems and processes for efficiency.  
Focus | Planning, scheduling, and quality control in production. | Optimizing resources, reducing waste, and improving productivity.  
Scope | Limited to production and manufacturing. | Applies to multiple industries (manufacturing, healthcare, logistics, etc.).  
Objective | Ensures smooth, cost-effective production. | Enhances overall efficiency and system performance.  

### 4. What is the relationship between productivity and profitability?
Higher productivity leads to lower costs and increased output, directly contributing to improved profitability.  

### 5. List two roles of an Industrial Engineer.
• Improve Efficiency  
• Ensure Quality  
• Enhance Safety  
• Reduce Waste  
• Optimize Resources  
• Automate Workflows  

### 6. What is meant by plant location?
Plant location refers to the selection of a geographical site for setting up an industrial facility.  

### 7. Enumerate the various types of Plant Layouts (or) List the types of plant layout.
• Product Layout  
• Process Layout  
• Fixed Position Layout  
• Cellular Layout.  

### 8. What are the key components of a production system?
The key components include  
• inputs  
• processes  
• output  
• feedback  
• control mechanisms.  

### 9. What is cellular layout?
Cellular layout organizes machines, equipment, and workers into small, self-contained units called cells. Each cell is set up to produce a specific part or group of similar products.  

### 10. List any two factors affecting plant location.
Two major factors are  
• availability of raw materials  
• proximity to markets  
• land  

### 11. Mention two advantages of a Product Layout.
• Efficient workflow  
• Short manufacturing cycle  
• Reduced material handling  
• Simplified production  
• Perfect line balancing  
• Reduced inventory.  

### 12. Define Industrial Engineering.
Industrial engineering is the optimization of systems, processes, and resources to improve productivity and efficiency. It integrates people, materials, information, equipment, energy and information.  

### 13. Who is the father of Industrial Engineering? Why is he called so?
Frederick Winslow Taylor is known as the father of Industrial Engineering due to his work on scientific management and efficiency improvement.  

### 14. What is the difference between efficiency and productivity?
Efficiency measures how well resources are used, while productivity measures output relative to input.  

### 15. What is a production system?
A production system is a framework for transforming raw materials into finished products.  

### 16. Write the importance of line balancing.
Line balancing reduces production bottlenecks and improves workflow continuity.  

### 17. List two factors affecting plant location.
Labor availability and transportation facilities.  

### 18. What is the difference between product layout and process layout?
Product Layout arranges machines in sequence of production flow, while Process Layout groups similar machines together.  

### 19. Mention two objectives of Plant Layout.
Maximizing production efficiency and reducing material handling costs.
